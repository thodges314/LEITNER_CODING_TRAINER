Write four formatters (no Intl): formatNumber(n, decimals = 0) inserts thousands commas ('1,234,567.89', negatives keep the sign); formatBytes(bytes, decimals = 1) uses 1024-based units B/KB/MB/GB/TB and drops trailing zeros ('1.5 KB', '1 MB', '0 B'); timeAgo(timestampMs, nowMs) returns 'just now' under 60 seconds, otherwise the largest whole unit (minute, hour, day, month = 30 days, year = 365 days) rounded, singular/plural ('1 hour ago', '5 minutes ago', 'in 3 hours' for the future); truncate(str, max) shortens to at most max characters including a trailing '…'.
