```ts
interface CarouselProps {
  slides: string[];
  loop?: boolean;
  autoPlayMs?: number;
}
```

Build an accessible image carousel component for an array of slide strings:

### Carousel({ slides, loop = true, autoPlayMs }: CarouselProps): JSX.Element
- **DOM Structure**: Render `<section aria-roledescription="carousel" aria-label="Gallery" tabIndex={0}>` showing **ONLY** the current slide inside `<div role="group" aria-roledescription="slide" aria-label="N of Total">` (1-based, e.g. `"1 of 3"`).
- **Navigation Controls**:
  - Provide `<button>Previous</button>` and `<button>Next</button>`. With `loop`, they wrap around; without `loop`, they are disabled at the ends.
  - Provide one dot button per slide with `aria-label="Go to slide N"` and `aria-current="true"` on the active dot only.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Pressing `ArrowRight` or `ArrowLeft` on the focused section navigates slides.
- **Auto-Play**: When `autoPlayMs` is set, advance every `autoPlayMs` milliseconds (respecting `loop`), and clear the timer on unmount.

#### Optional Styling (CSS is not graded)
Optional helper classes are available in the preview environment:
- `.carousel-slide`: styled slide container with height, centering, and soft background for `<div role="group">`.
You may use these classes, custom styles, or your own class names.
