Implement geometric two-pointer algorithms in O(n) time:

### `maxArea(heights)`
- Calculates the maximum area of water a container formed by two vertical lines at indices `i < j` can hold: min(heights[i], heights[j]) * (j - i).
- Operates in O(n) time using two pointers moving inward from both ends.
- Returns `0` if `heights.length < 2`.

### `trap(height)`
- Computes the total volume of rainwater trapped between unit-width elevation bars.
- Uses two pointers with `leftMax` and `rightMax` tracking in O(n) time and O(1) space.
- Returns `0` if `height.length < 3`.
