Build Wizard({ steps, onSubmit }) with steps [{ title, fields: [{ name, label }] }]. Render one <form> showing <p>Step X of N</p>, <h2> with the step title, and for each field a <label> wrapping a controlled <input name>. Provide <button type="button">Back</button> (disabled on the first step) and <button type="submit"> labelled 'Next' (or 'Submit' on the last step). Submitting the form (Enter or the button) validates the CURRENT step only: if any of its fields is empty after trimming, show <p role="alert">All fields are required</p> and stay; otherwise go to the next step, or on the last step call onSubmit(values) with ALL values entered across the steps. Values typed earlier must still be there after Back/Next. Moving between steps clears the alert.
