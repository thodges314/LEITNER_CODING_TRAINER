Build Stopwatch(). Show the elapsed time in <div role="timer" aria-label="Elapsed time"> formatted MM:SS.cc (minutes, seconds, hundredths, each zero-padded to two digits, e.g. 1234 ms -> "00:01.23", 61050 ms -> "01:01.05"). Compute the elapsed time from Date.now() (not by counting ticks) and re-render about every 10 ms while running. Buttons (all type="button"): a toggle labelled "Start" while stopped and "Pause" while running (pausing freezes the time; starting again resumes without counting the paused interval), "Lap" (disabled while not running) which appends the current time to <ol aria-label="Laps"> as <li>Lap 1 00:01.23</li>, <li>Lap 2 ...</li>, and "Reset" which stops, sets the time to 00:00.00 and clears the laps. Clear the interval whenever the watch is paused, reset or unmounted.
