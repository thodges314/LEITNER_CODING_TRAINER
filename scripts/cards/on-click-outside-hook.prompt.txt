### useOnClickOutside(ref: { current: HTMLElement | null }, handler: (event: Event) => void): void
Write `useOnClickOutside(ref, handler)`:

- **Trigger**: Calls `handler(event)` when a `mousedown` or `touchstart` event occurs anywhere outside `ref.current`.
- **Target Filtering**:
  - Ignores events originated inside `ref.current`.
  - Does nothing while `ref.current` is `null`.
- **Event Listeners**:
  - Attaches listeners to `document` and removes them on unmount.
  - Always calls the **LATEST** `handler` without re-subscribing on every render.
