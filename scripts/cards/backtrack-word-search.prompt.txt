Implement grid word-search backtracking on a 2D board of single-character strings:

### `exist(board: string[][], word: string): boolean`
- Checks whether `word` exists in the grid.
- The word can be constructed from letters of sequentially adjacent cells (horizontally or vertically neighboring).
- The same letter cell cannot be used more than once within the same path.
- Modifies board cells temporarily during exploration and restores them upon backtracking, leaving `board` unchanged.

### `findWords(board: string[][], words: string[]): string[]`
- Finds all distinct words from list `words` that exist on `board`.
- Each matching word should appear in the returned array once, in any order.
