Build DropdownMenu({ label, items, onSelect }) with items [{ id, label, disabled? }]. A trigger <button aria-haspopup="menu" aria-expanded> shows `label` and toggles the menu. While open render <ul role="menu"> with <li role="menuitem" tabIndex={-1}> items (disabled ones get aria-disabled="true"). Opening focuses the first ENABLED item. ArrowDown/ArrowUp move focus between enabled items and wrap; Home/End jump to the first/last enabled item; Enter or Space on an item selects it. Selecting (by click or key) calls onSelect(id), closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger; disabled items cannot be selected. Escape closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger; a mousedown outside the component closes it; Tab closes it.
