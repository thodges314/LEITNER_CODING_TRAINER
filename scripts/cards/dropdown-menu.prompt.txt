Build `DropdownMenu({ label, items, onSelect })` where `items` is `[{ id, label, disabled? }]`.

- **Trigger**: A trigger `<button aria-haspopup="menu" aria-expanded>` shows `label` and toggles the menu.
- **Menu**: While open, render `<ul role="menu">` with `<li role="menuitem" tabIndex={-1}>` items (disabled ones get `aria-disabled="true"`).
- **Keyboard Navigation**:
  - Opening focuses the first **enabled** item.
  - `ArrowDown` / `ArrowUp` move focus between enabled items (wrapping around ends; disabled items are skipped).
  - `Home` / `End` jump to the first / last enabled item.
  - `Enter` or `Space` selects the focused item.
- **Selection & Dismissal**:
  - Selecting (by click or key) calls `onSelect(id)`, closes the menu, and returns focus to the trigger (disabled items cannot be selected).
  - `Escape` closes the menu and returns focus to the trigger.
  - A `mousedown` outside the component or pressing `Tab` closes the menu.
