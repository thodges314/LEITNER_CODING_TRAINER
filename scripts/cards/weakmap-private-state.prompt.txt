Without using # fields, closures-per-instance or properties on the instance, keep each instance's state in module-level `WeakMap`s.

### `class Counter(start?: number)`
- Methods:
  - `increment(): this`
  - `decrement(): this`
  - `reset(): this` (resets back to the constructor's `start` value)
  - `get value(): number`

### `class Stack<T = unknown>()`
- Methods:
  - `push(x: T): void`
  - `pop(): T | undefined`
  - `peek(): T | undefined`
  - `get size(): number`

`Object.keys(instance)` must be `[]` and `JSON.stringify(instance)` must be `'{}'`.
