Build a robust, object-oriented event emitter class:

### `new EventEmitter(): EventEmitter`

- **`on(event, fn)`**:
  - Registers `fn` as a listener for `event`. Listeners execute in registration order.
- **`off(event, fn)`**:
  - Removes the specific listener `fn` from `event`.
  - Can remove a listener registered via `once(event, fn)` prior to it firing.
- **`once(event, fn)`**:
  - Registers a one-time listener that automatically unregisters itself upon its first execution.
- **`emit(event, ...args)`**:
  - Invokes all listeners currently registered for `event` with `...args`.
  - Emitting an event with no listeners does nothing.
  - Safe against listener mutation: removing or unsubscribing a listener during an active `emit` snapshot must not skip other listeners.
