Implement dynamic programming variants on 1D arrays:

### `robCircular(nums: number[]): number`
- Calculates the maximum loot from houses arranged in a circle (where the first and last houses are adjacent) such that no two adjacent houses are robbed.
- Returns `0` for an empty array, and `nums[0]` for a single-element array.

### `maxProduct(nums: number[]): number`
- Finds the largest product of a non-empty contiguous subarray in integer array `nums`.
- Handles negative numbers and zeros by tracking running minimum and maximum products.

### `minCostClimbingStairs(cost: number[]): number`
- Calculates the minimum cost to reach the top of a floor staircase (just past the last step).
- You can start at either step index `0` or `1`, and paying `cost[i]` allows climbing 1 or 2 steps forward.
