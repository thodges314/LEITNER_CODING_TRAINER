Write robCircular(nums): houses are in a circle (the first and last are adjacent), you cannot rob two adjacent houses; return the maximum loot (0 for an empty array, nums[0] for one house). Write maxProduct(nums): the largest product of a non-empty contiguous subarray (track both the running max and min because negatives flip them). Write minCostClimbingStairs(cost): you may start on step 0 or 1, pay cost[i] to leave step i and climb 1 or 2 steps; return the minimum cost to reach the top (just past the last index).
