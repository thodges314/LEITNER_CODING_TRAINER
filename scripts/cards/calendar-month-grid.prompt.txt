Implement the calendar grid generation logic for date pickers and monthly calendars:

### `buildMonthGrid(year: number, month: number, weekStartsOn: number = 0): (number | null)[][]`
- Generates a 2D array of weeks representing the month grid.
- **Parameters**:
  - `year`: 4-digit calendar year (leap years follow Gregorian rules: 2000 and 2024 are leap years, 1900 and 2023 are not).
  - `month`: 0-indexed month (`0` = January, `9` = October, `11` = December).
  - `weekStartsOn`: 0-indexed starting weekday for each row (`0` = Sunday, `1` = Monday, ..., `6` = Saturday).
- **Structure**:
  - Returns an array of week arrays; every week contains exactly 7 cells.
  - Inside-month days are positive day numbers (`1, 2, 3, ...`).
  - Leading and trailing padding cells are `null`.
  - All weeks are complete 7-day rows with no empty extra weeks.
