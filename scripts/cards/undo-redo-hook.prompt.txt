Write useHistory(initial, limit = 100) returning { state, set, undo, redo, canUndo, canRedo }. set(valueOrUpdater) pushes a new present state (an updater function receives the current state); setting a value that is Object.is-equal to the current one does nothing. undo() moves to the previous state and redo() back; both do nothing at the ends. Calling set() after undo discards the redo branch. At most `limit` past states are kept (the oldest are dropped). set/undo/redo keep a stable identity.
