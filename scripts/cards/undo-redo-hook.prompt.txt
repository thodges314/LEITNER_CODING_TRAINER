Write `useHistory(initial, limit = 100)`:

- **Returns**: `{ state, set, undo, redo, canUndo, canRedo }`.
- **`set(valueOrUpdater)`**:
  - Pushes a new present state (an updater function receives the current `state`).
  - Setting a value that is `Object.is`-equal to the current state is a no-op.
  - Calling `set()` after `undo()` discards the redo branch.
- **`undo()` & `redo()`**:
  - `undo()` moves to the previous state; `redo()` moves forward to the next state.
  - Both are no-ops when already at the boundaries (`canUndo: false` or `canRedo: false`).
- **History Capping**: At most `limit` past states are retained (the oldest are dropped once the limit is exceeded).
- **Identity**: `set`, `undo`, and `redo` keep stable function identities across renders.
