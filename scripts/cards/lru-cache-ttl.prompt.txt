Implement class LRUCacheTTL(capacity) with get(key) and set(key, value, ttlMs?), both in O(1). get returns the value, or -1 if the key is missing or has expired (expired entries are removed); a successful get marks the entry as most recently used. set inserts or overwrites an entry (making it the most recent) and evicts the least recently used entry when over capacity. ttlMs is optional: omitted (or 0) means the entry never expires.
