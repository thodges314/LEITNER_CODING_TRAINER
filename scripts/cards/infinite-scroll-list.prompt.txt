Build an infinite scroll list with an intersection sentinel:

### InfiniteList({ items = [], onLoadMore, hasMore, loading }: { items?: string[]; onLoadMore: () => void; hasMore: boolean; loading: boolean }): JSX.Element:
- Render `items` in a list (`<ul>` with one `<li>` per item) followed by a sentinel element observed with an `IntersectionObserver`.
- The sentinel is `<li ref={sentinelRef} data-sentinel aria-hidden="true" />`.
- When the sentinel intersects (becomes visible), call `onLoadMore()`.
- Do not call `onLoadMore()` while `loading` is `true`.
- When `hasMore` is `false`, stop loading and disconnect the observer.
- Always disconnect the observer on unmount.
