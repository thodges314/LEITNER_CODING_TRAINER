Prevent needless re-renders. Write List({ items, onSelect, onRender }) wrapped in React.memo: it renders a <ul> with one <li> per item string (clicking an <li> calls onSelect(item)) and calls onRender() every time it renders. Write Parent({ items, onListRender }): it has a counter button labelled `Count: N` (increments on click), shows `Selected: <item or none>`, and renders <List items={items} onSelect={...} onRender={onListRender} />. Clicking the counter or selecting an item must NOT re-render List; passing a new items array must.
