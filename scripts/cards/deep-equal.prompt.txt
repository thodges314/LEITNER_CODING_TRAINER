Implement deep equality comparison supporting primitives, structural objects, and edge cases:

### `deepEqual(a: unknown, b: unknown): boolean`
- **Primitives & NaN**:
  - Compares primitives by value (`a === b`).
  - Treats `NaN` as equal to `NaN`.
- **Structural Integrity**:
  - Treats arrays and plain objects with matching contents as different (checks prototype / constructor equality).
  - For `Date` instances, compares their underlying timestamps (`.getTime()`).
- **Objects & Arrays**:
  - Compares key counts and key sets; returns `false` if keys differ.
  - Recursively compares all property values.
- **Circular References**:
  - Safely handles circular references (e.g., via `WeakMap` or tracking sets) without infinite recursion.
