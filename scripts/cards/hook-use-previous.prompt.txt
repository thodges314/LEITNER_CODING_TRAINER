Write a custom hook to track a value from the previous render:

### `usePrevious(value)`
- Uses `useRef()` and `useEffect()` to store and track `value`.
- **Render Order**:
  - `ref.current` is returned during the render phase.
  - `useEffect` updates `ref.current = value` after paint, ensuring subsequent renders observe this value.
- Returns `undefined` on the initial mount render.
