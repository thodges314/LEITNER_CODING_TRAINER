Build TicTacToe({ onGameEnd }). Render <p role="status"> with "Next player: X" (X always starts, then O, alternating), "Winner: X" / "Winner: O" once a line of three is complete, or "Draw" when the board is full without a winner. Below it render <div role="group" aria-label="Tic-tac-toe board"> holding nine <button type="button"> cells with aria-label "Cell 1" ... "Cell 9" (row by row, left to right) whose text is the mark ("X", "O" or empty). Clicking an empty cell places the current player's mark; clicking an occupied cell, or any cell after the game has ended, does nothing. When the game ends call onGameEnd exactly once with "X", "O" or "draw". A <button type="button"> with the text "Restart" clears the board and gives X the first move again. Derive the winner from the board instead of storing it.
