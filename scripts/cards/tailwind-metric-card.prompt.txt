#### Card Structure & Tailwind Styling

- **Card Container** (`<div>`):
  - Constrained max width (`sm`), rounded corners, generous padding, dark/neutral card surface background, subtle border, and drop shadow.
- **Header Row** (`<div>`):
  - Flex row with centered items spaced apart:
  - Metric title (`<span>`): `"Total Revenue"` in small, medium-weight muted text.
  - Trend badge (`<span>`): `"+12.5%"` styled as a pill with extra-small semibold text, translucent emerald/green background, emerald/green text, and subtle matching border.
- **Metric Value** (`<div>`):
  - `"$48,250"` with top margin, large bold/extrabold display typography, tight tracking, and high-contrast text.
- **Comparison Subtext** (`<p>`):
  - `"vs. $42,890 last month"` with small top margin and extra-small muted text.
- **Action Button** (`<button type="button">`):
  - `"View Detailed Report"` styled full width with top margin, vertical/horizontal padding, rounded corners, indigo/blue primary background with white medium text, hover state, color transition, and focus ring.
