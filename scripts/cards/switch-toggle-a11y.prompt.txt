Build `Switch({ checked, onChange, disabled = false, children })`, a controlled accessible on/off switch:

- **DOM Structure**: Render a real `<button type="button">` (ensuring native `Space` and `Enter` activation without submitting parent forms).
- **Accessibility & ARIA**:
  - `role="switch"`
  - `aria-checked` set to the string `"true"` or `"false"` matching `checked`.
  - `children` provide both the visible label and the accessible name.
- **Interactions**:
  - Clicking calls `onChange` with the flipped value: `onChange(!checked)`.
  - When `disabled={true}`, the `<button>` is disabled and clicks do nothing.
