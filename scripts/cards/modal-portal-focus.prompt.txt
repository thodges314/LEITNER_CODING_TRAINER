Create Modal({ isOpen, onClose, children }) rendered with ReactDOM.createPortal into document.body (render nothing when closed). Structure: a backdrop element containing a role='dialog' aria-modal='true' element with the children. Escape calls onClose; clicking the backdrop (but not the dialog content) calls onClose; Tab/Shift+Tab are trapped inside the dialog (wrap around the first/last focusable element); focus moves into the dialog on open and returns to the previously focused element (the trigger) on close; document.body.style.overflow is 'hidden' while open and restored afterwards.
