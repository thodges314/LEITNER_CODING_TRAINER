Write combinationSum(candidates, target): all unique combinations of distinct positive numbers summing to target, where each number may be reused any number of times.

Write combinationSum2(candidates, target): candidates may contain duplicates and each element may be used at most once; the result must contain no duplicate combinations (sort first and skip repeated values at the same depth).

Write combinationSum3(k, n): all combinations of exactly k distinct digits from 1 to 9 that sum to n. Result order does not matter, and numbers inside each combination should be ascending.
