Implement interval overlap detection and total span calculations for closed intervals `[start, end]` (where `start <= end` and touching endpoints overlap):

### `overlaps(a, b)`
- Returns `true` if closed intervals `a` and `b` overlap or touch at their boundaries; otherwise `false`.

### `intersection(a, b)`
- Returns the overlapping closed interval `[start, end]` shared by `a` and `b`.
- Returns `null` if they do not overlap.

### `totalLength(intervals)`
- Computes the combined length (`end - start`) of all intervals in `intervals`, counting overlapping spans only once.
- Safely handles unsorted inputs.
- Returns `0` for empty intervals.
