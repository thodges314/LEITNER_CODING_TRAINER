Write minWindow(s, t): the smallest substring of s that contains every character of t with at least its multiplicity (the leftmost one on ties); '' if none exists or t is empty.

Write checkInclusion(s1, s2): true when s2 contains some permutation of s1 as a contiguous substring.

Write findAnagrams(s, p): the array of start indices (ascending) of every substring of s that is an anagram of p ([] when p is longer than s).
