Build Tooltip({ content, children }) where children is a single element (usually a button). Clone the child so it keeps its own props and handlers but also: shows the tooltip on mouse enter and on focus, hides it on mouse leave, on blur and when Escape is pressed. While visible render <span role="tooltip" id={id}>{content}</span> next to the trigger (content may be any node) and give the trigger aria-describedby={id}; while hidden render no tooltip and no aria-describedby. Use useId() for the id. The child's own onMouseEnter, onMouseLeave, onFocus, onBlur and onKeyDown handlers must still be called.
