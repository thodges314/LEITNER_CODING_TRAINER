Implement asynchronous timing helpers using Promises:

### `sleep(ms: number): Promise<void>`
- Returns a Promise that resolves (with `undefined`) after `ms` milliseconds via `setTimeout`.

### `delay<T>(value: T, ms: number): Promise<T>`
- Resolves with `value` after `ms` milliseconds.

### `measure(fn: () => any | Promise<any>): Promise<number>`
- Awaits `fn()` and returns the elapsed time in milliseconds as a number.
