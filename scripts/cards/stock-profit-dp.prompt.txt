Implement state-machine and greedy dynamic programming for stock trading:

### `maxProfit(prices: number[]): number`
- Calculates the maximum profit achievable from at most one transaction (one buy followed by one later sell).
- Returns `0` if no profit is possible or if `prices` is empty.

### `maxProfitMany(prices: number[]): number`
- Calculates the maximum total profit with unlimited buy and sell transactions (holding at most one share at any time).
- Accumulates profits from every upward price movement.

### `maxProfitCooldown(prices: number[]): number`
- Calculates the maximum profit with unlimited transactions subject to a one-day cooldown period (cannot buy the day immediately following a sell).
- Models state transitions between `hold`, `sold`, and `rest`.
